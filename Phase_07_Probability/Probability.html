🎲 PROBABILITY - Complete Guide

What is Probability?

Probability measures how likely an event is to happen.

It's a number between 0 (impossible) and 1 (certain).
0 = Impossible Will never happen

Example: Rolling 7 on a die
0.5 = Equally Likely 50-50 chance

Example: Coin flip
1 = Certain Will always happen

Example: Sun rises tomorrow
P(Event) = Number of Favorable Outcomes ÷ Total Possible Outcomes

Basic Probability

Key Terms:
• Experiment: An action with uncertain outcome (rolling dice)
• Outcome: A possible result (getting a 4)
• Sample Space: All possible outcomes (1,2,3,4,5,6)
• Event: One or more outcomes (getting even number)

Example 1: Rolling a Die

🎲 What is the probability of rolling a 4?

1Sample Space: {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}
Total outcomes = 6
2Favorable outcomes: {4}
Count = 1
3Calculate:
P(4) = 1/6 = 0.167 or 16.7%
P(rolling 4) = 1/6 ≈ 16.7%

Example 2: Drawing Cards

🃏 What is the probability of drawing a King from a deck?

1Total cards: 52
2Kings in deck: 4 (one per suit)
3Calculate:
P(King) = 4/52 = 1/13 = 0.077 or 7.7%
P(King) = 4/52 = 1/13 ≈ 7.7%

Types of Events

1. Independent Events

Events where one outcome does NOT affect the other.
Example: Flipping a coin twice - first flip doesn't affect second.
P(A AND B) = P(A) × P(B)

Multiply the probabilities!

📝 Probability of getting heads twice in a row?

1P(Heads) = 1/2
2P(Heads AND Heads) = 1/2 × 1/2 = 1/4 = 25%
P(HH) = 1/4 = 25%

2. Dependent Events

Events where one outcome AFFECTS the other.
Example: Drawing cards without replacement.

📝 Draw 2 Kings in a row (without replacement)?

1P(1st King) = 4/52
2P(2nd King | 1st was King) = 3/51 (one less King, one less card)
3P(Both Kings) = 4/52 × 3/51 = 12/2652 = 1/221 ≈ 0.45%
P(Two Kings) ≈ 0.45%

3. Mutually Exclusive Events

Events that cannot happen at the same time.
Example: Rolling a 2 AND a 5 on one die roll (impossible!)
P(A OR B) = P(A) + P(B)

Add the probabilities! (when mutually exclusive)

📝 Probability of rolling 2 OR 5?

1P(2) = 1/6
2P(5) = 1/6
3P(2 OR 5) = 1/6 + 1/6 = 2/6 = 1/3 ≈ 33.3%
P(2 or 5) = 1/3 ≈ 33.3%

Complement Rule

The complement of an event is "everything else" (event NOT happening).
Probabilities of event and its complement always add up to 1.
P(NOT A) = 1 - P(A)

P(A) + P(NOT A) = 1

📝 Probability of NOT rolling a 6?

1P(6) = 1/6
2P(NOT 6) = 1 - 1/6 = 5/6 ≈ 83.3%
P(NOT 6) = 5/6 ≈ 83.3%
✨ Tip: Use complement when it's easier to calculate "NOT happening"!
Example: P(at least one head in 3 flips) = 1 - P(no heads) = 1 - (1/2)³ = 7/8

Addition Rule (OR)

For events that CAN happen together (not mutually exclusive):
P(A OR B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A AND B)

Subtract overlap to avoid counting twice!

📝 P(King OR Heart) from a deck?

1P(King) = 4/52
2P(Heart) = 13/52
3P(King AND Heart) = 1/52 (King of Hearts)
4P(King OR Heart) = 4/52 + 13/52 - 1/52 = 16/52 = 4/13
P(King or Heart) = 16/52 ≈ 30.8%

Conditional Probability

Probability of A given that B has happened.
Written as P(A|B) - "Probability of A given B"
P(A|B) = P(A AND B) ÷ P(B)

📝 Given a card is red, what's P(it's a King)?

1Red cards: 26 (hearts + diamonds)
2Red Kings: 2 (King of Hearts, King of Diamonds)
3P(King | Red) = 2/26 = 1/13 ≈ 7.7%
P(King | Red) = 1/13 ≈ 7.7%

Expected Value

The average outcome you'd expect over many trials.
Useful for games, gambling, risk analysis.
E(X) = Σ (Value × Probability)

Multiply each outcome by its probability, then add all up

📝 Expected value of rolling a die?

Each outcome (1-6) has P = 1/6
E(X) = 1×(1/6) + 2×(1/6) + 3×(1/6) + 4×(1/6) + 5×(1/6) + 6×(1/6)
E(X) = (1+2+3+4+5+6)/6 = 21/6 = 3.5
Expected Value = 3.5

📌 KEY POINTS

✓ P(Event): Favorable ÷ Total outcomes (0 to 1)
✓ P(A AND B): P(A) × P(B) for independent
✓ P(A OR B): P(A) + P(B) - P(A AND B)
✓ Complement: P(NOT A) = 1 - P(A)
✓ Conditional: P(A|B) = P(A AND B) ÷ P(B)
✓ Expected Value: Σ(Value × Probability)
